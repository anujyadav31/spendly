Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what Spendly needs to work:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.
  • Basic technical information, such as your browser type and when you last signed in.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to run your account, show your expenses, and build the summaries and category breakdowns you see in the app. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers and is accessible only to your account. Passwords are never stored in plain text. If you delete an expense or your account, the related data is removed from our active systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your expense data with third parties. We may use trusted providers for things like hosting and fonts, and these providers only receive the technical information needed to deliver their service.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to request a copy or deletion of your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
